## 2. The information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, information created when you use StageViva, and limited technical information needed to provide the service.

### Account information

This can include your name, email address, account identifier and membership status. If you sign in through a third-party sign-in service, we receive the account information that service makes available to StageViva.

### Your CV and performer profile

When you upload a CV, we store the file and use its contents to create and improve your Artist DNA profile. A CV may contain professional history, training, repertoire, skills, availability, location, nationality, contact details, and other information that you choose to include.

Your Artist DNA includes structured information drawn from your CV and answers, such as disciplines, experience, training, availability and work preferences. You can review and edit the parts of your performer profile that StageViva makes editable.

If a PDF CV contains a clear professional headshot, StageViva may extract it for your private profile. It is not made public by StageViva and you can ask us to remove it.

### Matching and preferences

We keep your answers to profile questions, match scores, opportunity recommendations, saved or viewed matching data where available, and alert preferences. We may also store a browser or device push-notification subscription if you choose to enable phone notifications.

### Support communications

If you contact us, we keep the information in your message and our reply so we can support you and resolve the issue.

## 3. How we use your information

We use personal data to:

- create and manage your StageViva account;
- analyse your CV and answers to build and improve your Artist DNA;
- identify, rank and show opportunities that may suit your profile;
- provide profile editing, membership features and account settings;
- send in-app, phone or email alerts that you have enabled;
- keep StageViva secure, prevent misuse, troubleshoot problems and improve the service; and
- respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Our legal bases for using your data

Where UK GDPR or similar privacy law applies, we normally rely on:

- **Contract:** where processing is necessary to provide the StageViva service you request, including account management, CV analysis and matching;
- **Legitimate interests:** to secure, maintain, improve and protect StageViva, provided your rights and interests do not override ours; and
- **Consent:** for optional phone notifications and any other processing where we specifically ask for it. You can withdraw consent at any time, including through your device or app settings.

## 5. AI and automated matching

StageViva uses AI tools to turn the information in your CV into a structured performer profile and to help assess how closely an opportunity aligns with that profile. This is profiling for recommendation purposes.

StageViva does not make hiring decisions, submit applications on your behalf, or decide whether you may work for an employer. Employers and artists make their own decisions. You can edit your profile, review recommendations, and contact us at stageviva@gmail.com if you believe information or a recommendation is inaccurate.

## 6. Who we share information with

We use carefully selected service providers to operate StageViva. They process personal data only as needed to provide their services to us. These may include:

- **Cloudflare**, which helps provide and protect the StageViva website and domain;
- **Render**, which hosts the StageViva application service and its encrypted-in-transit data storage;
- **Lovable Cloud / Supabase**, where used to authenticate users and operate the web application;
- **OpenAI**, which helps analyse CV content and, where applicable, select an appropriate headshot from a CV PDF;
- **Resend or another email delivery provider**, if you enable email notifications; and
- **push-notification providers and your device/browser platform**, if you enable phone notifications.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a genuine business reorganisation. We do not sell your personal information or use it for behavioural advertising.

## 7. International transfers

Some of our providers may process information outside the UK, European Economic Area or your country. When that happens, we use appropriate safeguards required by applicable privacy law, such as adequacy regulations or approved contractual protections.

## 8. How long we keep information

We keep your account, profile and CV while your account remains active and for as long as needed to provide StageViva. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymise the associated active data unless we need to retain something for legal, security or dispute-resolution reasons.

Secure system backups may retain a limited copy temporarily and are normally rotated within **14 days**. We may keep support correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the request and maintain appropriate records.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data, including access controls and secure connections. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please keep your account credentials private and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.
