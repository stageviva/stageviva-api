# StageViva Opportunity Quality Standard

## Purpose

StageViva is a career-opportunity service for performing artists. Every live
listing must help an artist make a credible, current application decision. It
is not a general events calendar, school directory, or class finder.

## What may be live

A listing may be live only when it is one of the following:

1. A paid professional contract, engagement, casting, or company vacancy.
2. A genuine company trainee, apprentice, young-artist, graduate-company, or
   pre-professional pathway that is explicitly connected to professional work.
3. A legitimate agency or representation call for professional performers.

The opportunity must be current, attributable to a credible organisation or
source, and include a public official application or careers destination. A
directory is useful for discovery but is never the final application link.

## What must never be live

- General dance schools, academies, conservatoires, courses, degrees, classes,
  workshops, masterclasses, intensives, summer schools, or tuition.
- Competitions, showcases, festivals, networking events, or unpaid volunteer
  work unless the listing is an explicit professional paid casting/contract.
- Expired deadlines, past audition dates, or closed seasons.
- Vague listings that do not establish a role, career pathway, or credible
  organisation.
- Listings whose only link is a third-party directory when no official
  destination has been verified.

## Required live-listing information

Before an opportunity is shown to performers, StageViva should have:

- **Company / organisation** — the hiring company, producer, casting office or
  agency; never a directory name.
- **Role summary** — plain language such as `Company dancer`, `Female ensemble
  dancer`, `Young artist programme`, or `Talent representation`.
- **Location** — city and country when available; otherwise clearly state that
  it is remote, touring, or to be confirmed.
- **Deadline** — exact date where supplied, otherwise an honest `ASAP` only
  when the official listing says so.
- **Official application URL** — the company, casting office, or agency page.
- **Contract / pathway type** — contract, freelance/project, trainee,
  apprenticeship, pre-professional pathway, cruise/resort, commercial/screen,
  or agency representation.
- **Description** — concise factual explanation of the actual opportunity.

Unknown fields are acceptable only where the official listing genuinely does
not state them. StageViva must never invent compensation, dates, requirements,
or eligibility.

## Naming standard

Opportunity cards use this format:

**Company / organisation**

**Role summary**

Examples:

- `Birmingham Royal Ballet` — `Company dancer audition`
- `Brisbane Ballet` — `Trainee and company dancer positions`
- `Royal Caribbean Productions` — `Male dancer / singer contract`
- `Example Talent Agency` — `Professional dancer representation`

Never show scraped headline fragments, dates, gender requirements or address
blocks as the company name. Never use the generic label `Performance
opportunity` when a meaningful role can be extracted.

## Categories and filters

Every live listing has one career track and one or more style categories.

**Career track:** Contracts, Trainee / apprenticeship, or Agency.

**Style categories:** Ballet, Contemporary, Jazz, Commercial, Musical theatre,
Tap, Cruise/resort, Acting, Singing, Modelling, Circus / acro.

An agency submission belongs in the separate **Agency** track. It must not be
presented as a paid contract unless that specific agency listing offers one.

## Matching integrity

- A score reflects evidence in the artist profile and the opportunity—not a
  generic industry assumption.
- Required singing, acting, jazz, musical theatre, gender, work-rights or
  height criteria must lower the match when the artist lacks the evidence.
- A clearly incompatible explicit requirement may remain visible only as a low
  score with an honest explanation; it must never be recommended or notified.
- StageViva must not rate someone highly for a discipline merely because they
  are highly skilled in a different one.

## Daily review routine

1. Discover candidate listings from approved sources.
2. Extract structured facts from the listing.
3. Reject expired, educational, vague, or non-career listings automatically.
4. Find and save the official application destination.
5. Standardise company name, role, track and categories.
6. Match only eligible current listings.
7. Surface anything incomplete or uncertain in Content Review rather than
   publishing it automatically.

## Creator review decisions

**Live:** Meets every requirement and official link is verified.

**Hide:** Inaccurate, expired, school/course content, duplicate, wrong source,
or no longer actionable. Hidden entries remain available to restore.

**Manual addition:** Only add a current professional opportunity from its
official page. Complete every required field before publishing it.
